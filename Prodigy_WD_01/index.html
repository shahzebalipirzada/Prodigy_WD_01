About

Comfort-focused food with a calm, modern feel.

Tandoori Tadka is designed around familiar Indian dishes, clean presentation, and a relaxed dining flow. The menu is intentionally small so the kitchen can focus on consistency, freshness, and balanced spice.

It works as a neighborhood restaurant concept, a takeaway page, or a portfolio-ready front end for a small business.

Kitchen style Tandoor, grill, and seasonal prep
Service style Casual dine-in and easy takeaway
Dining feel Warm, minimal, and unhurried
